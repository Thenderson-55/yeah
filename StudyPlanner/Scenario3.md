## Scenario Details

- **Primary actor:** Maya Johnson
- **Goal:** Divide a large project into smaller, manageable tasks
- **Context:** Maya has a major class project due in two weeks
- **Preconditions:** Maya has added the project and its final due date to the Study Planner

## Narrative

Maya sees that a major project is due in two weeks. The project feels difficult to manage because it has several parts. She uses the Study Planner to make a list of smaller tasks, such as choosing a topic, finding sources, creating an outline, writing a draft, and proofreading. She assigns a target date to each task so she does not have to complete everything at the last minute. As she finishes each part, she marks it as complete. The smaller steps make the project feel more manageable and help Maya keep making progress.
