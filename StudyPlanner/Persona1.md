## Persona Details

- **Age:** 18
- **Occupation:** First-year college student
- **Education:** Recently graduated from high school
- **Technology experience:** Comfortable using a smartphone, laptop, and common school websites
- **Goals:** Keep track of assignments, remember due dates, plan study sessions, and avoid turning work in late
- **Needs:** A simple place to see upcoming assignments and organize schoolwork by class
- **Frustrations:** Due dates are spread across different course websites, and it is easy to forget smaller assignments
- **Motivations:** Stay organized, earn good grades, and balance school with friends and activities

## Narrative

Maya is adjusting to college and is taking several classes that each use a different course page. She checks her phone often, but she sometimes misses an assignment because its due date was posted in a different place from her other classwork. She wants a study planner that lets her enter assignments, set due dates, and see what needs to be completed next. She prefers a clear layout that does not take a long time to learn. If she can break a busy week into smaller tasks and see her progress, she feels more confident about staying on top of her classes.
