## Scenario Details

- **Primary actor:** Daniel Brooks
- **Goal:** Adjust his study plan when his work schedule changes
- **Context:** Daniel is scheduled to work during a time he had planned to study
- **Preconditions:** Daniel has assignments and study sessions saved in the Study Planner

## Narrative

Daniel checks his Study Planner and sees that he planned to work on an assignment after work on Wednesday. His manager changes his shift, and Daniel realizes he will not have enough time that evening. He opens the planner, reviews the other days in his schedule, and moves the study session to Thursday. He checks the assignment's due date to make sure the new study time still gives him enough time to finish. The updated plan helps Daniel keep track of his responsibilities even when his availability changes.
