## Narrative

Daniel works during the day and takes college classes part-time. Most evenings, he has only a short amount of time available for studying. When his work schedule changes, he sometimes has to rearrange his school plans and can lose track of what needs to be done first. He wants a study planner that shows assignment due dates and lets him schedule study time around his other responsibilities. He also wants to move tasks to a different day without losing track of them. A flexible planner would help him use his limited time wisely and make steady progress toward his degree.
