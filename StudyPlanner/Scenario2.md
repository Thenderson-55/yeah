## Scenario Details

- **Primary actor:** Maya Johnson
- **Goal:** Keep track of several assignments that are due close together
- **Context:** Multiple classes have quizzes, homework, and projects due during the same week
- **Preconditions:** Maya has entered her assignments and their due dates into the Study Planner

## Narrative

Maya opens the Study Planner and looks at the assignments coming up over the next several days. She notices that a quiz, a homework assignment, and a project are all due before Friday. Instead of trying to remember everything, she reviews the due dates and decides which tasks to work on first. She marks completed work as finished and checks the remaining assignments. Seeing all of the deadlines together helps Maya avoid forgetting an assignment and gives her a clearer idea of how to use her study time.
