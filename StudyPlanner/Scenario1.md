## Scenario Details

- **Primary actor:** Maya Johnson
- **Goal:** Organize assignments and plan study time for the week
- **Context:** Maya is starting a new school week and has assignments in several classes
- **Preconditions:** Maya has access to the Study Planner and knows the assignments she needs to complete

## Narrative

On Sunday evening, Maya opens the Study Planner to get ready for the upcoming week. She enters the assignments she sees in each of her course pages and adds the class name and due date for each one. The planner displays her assignments in order by due date, so she can see which work needs attention first. Maya reviews the week and chooses times to study for each class. After saving her plan, she can return to it during the week to check what she needs to complete next.
